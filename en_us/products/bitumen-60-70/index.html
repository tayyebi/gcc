Bitumen 60/70 – Penetration Grade Paving Bitumen

Product Overview

Bitumen 60/70 is a penetration grade paving bitumen produced from the vacuum distillation of crude oil. It is one of the most commonly used grades worldwide due to its balanced hardness, high adhesion, and excellent performance in moderate to warm climates.

This grade provides optimal workability during mixing and laying, while maintaining durability and resistance under traffic load.

What Does 60/70 Mean?

The designation 60/70 refers to the penetration value of the bitumen: - Measured using a standard needle - At 25°C - Under a load of 100 g for 5 seconds - Penetration range: 60–70 dmm (0.1 mm)

Lower penetration = harder bitumen
Higher penetration = softer bitumen

Bitumen 60/70 offers a medium hardness, ideal for paving applications.

Key Advantages

  • High bonding strength with aggregates
  • Excellent resistance to deformation and rutting
  • Stable performance under heavy traffic
  • Good waterproofing properties
  • Proven long-term durability
  • Globally accepted paving grade

Applications

Road & Highway Construction

  • Asphalt concrete for highways, urban roads, and rural roads
  • Airport runways and taxiways
  • Parking areas and industrial pavements

Asphalt Production

  • Hot mix asphalt (HMA)
  • Dense-graded asphalt mixtures

Waterproofing & Construction

  • Bituminous membranes
  • Damp-proofing layers
  • Base material for modified bitumen products

Climatic Suitability

  • Best performance in moderate to warm climates
  • Suitable for regions with high traffic loads
  • Not recommended for extremely cold climates without modification
