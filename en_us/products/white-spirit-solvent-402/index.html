White Spirit (Solvent 402)

2026-02-07

White Spirit, also known as Mineral Spirits or Solvent 402, is a petroleum-derived clear liquid used widely as a common organic solvent in painting and decorating. It represents a mixture of aliphatic and alicyclic hydrocarbons with a limited aromatic content, offering excellent solvency power with a controlled evaporation rate.

Product Overview

Feature Detail
Formal Name White Spirit (Mineral Spirits, Solvent 402)
Common Trade Names White Spirit, Mineral Spirits
HS Code 2710.12
Chemical Formula Hydrocarbon mixture (~C₇–C₁₂)

Key Applications

  • Paints & Coatings: The most common solvent for oil-based paints, varnishes, and staining products.
  • Degreasing Agent: Effective for cleaning machinery, tools, and automotive parts.
  • Dry Cleaning: Used in some dry cleaning processes.
  • Additives: Component in wood preservatives, lacquers, and aerosol products.

Technical Specifications

Our Solvent 402 is manufactured to meet rigorous industry standards:

  • Stability: Chemically stable and non-corrosive.
  • Odor: Low odor grades available for indoor applications.
  • Flash Point: Controlled to balance safety (flammability) and drying time.
  • Sulfur Content: Ultra-low sulfur to prevent corrosion and environmental impact.

Logistics & Packaging

  • Drummed: 175 kg or 200 liter new steel drums.
  • Bulk ISO Tanks: For large scale industrial consumers.
  • Flexi-bags: Cost-effective option for non-hazardous variant transport where applicable.
