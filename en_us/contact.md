# Contact Us

## Get in Touch

We'd love to hear from you. Whether you have a question about our services, need support, or want to explore partnership opportunities, our team is here to help.

### Contact Information

**Email**: info@globalcommercecouncil.com  
**Hours**: Monday - Friday, 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM EST

### Send Us a Message

Write to **info@globalcommercecouncil.com** and include:

- **Name** and **company**
- **Email** address for the reply
- **Subject** — general inquiry, supplier application, buyer inquiry, partnership opportunity, technical support, or something else
- **Message** — the product or service you are asking about, plus any quantity, destination or deadline we should know about

Every message gets a reply.
